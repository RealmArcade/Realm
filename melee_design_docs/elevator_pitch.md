# Elevator Pitch

Status: Working Draft

## One Line

A real-time strategy game where you control one hero, build a base and an army, and win by destroying the enemy's Nexus.

## The Pitch

Realm is a real-time strategy game in which you directly control one unit: your hero.
Soldiers are drafted from a shared pool, rather than chosen as a predefined faction.
Economy, unit production queues, and combat are simplified so you can focus on the macro.

You select your Hero in the lobby. All four hero abilities are unlocked from the start of the match.
Wood buys buildings, which unlock soldiers and economy.
Gold buys relics: up to six maximum, which are player-wide buffs to your whole composition.
Neutral monsters, player kills, and building destruction award experience and resource bounties; Experience raises your army's level.
Resource locations, terrain, and chokepoints determine where players expand, fight, and retreat.
Destroy your opponent's Nexus to win.
