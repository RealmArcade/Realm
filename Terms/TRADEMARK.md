trademarks, brand names, or visual identity.

### What You May Do
* Use our name and logo when giving attribution, by stating that your game or UGC was "built using Realm Engine".

* Naming your fork or standalone game "Realm", or any title confusingly similar.
* Using the Realm name, logo or the Steam store page's presentation as your own product's name or branding.
