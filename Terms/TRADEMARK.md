# Realm Trademark & Brand Guidelines

The source code of "Realm Engine" is licensed under the MIT License, but this does not include a license to use our
trademarks, brand names, or visual identity. "Realm" is also the name of the official game on Steam, and that store page is
covered by the same rules as the name itself.

### What You May Do
* Use our name and logo when giving attribution, by stating that your game or UGC was "built using Realm Engine".
* Link to the original Steam Workshop item when you publish a mirror or a derivative of a map.
* Build a map that is inspired by another map. Ideas, rules, mechanics and pacing are not what the UGC License protects —
  only the expression of them. A similar map built from scratch, copying nobody's assets, scripts or text and asking
  nobody's permission, is permitted and lawful, and the editor's reverse-to-prompt workflow is a normal way to make one.

### What Requires Permission
* Naming your fork or standalone game "Realm", "Realm Engine", or any title confusingly similar.
* Using the Realm name, logo or the Steam store page's presentation as your own product's name or branding.
* Implying affiliation, sponsorship, or endorsement by Realm, by the official game or by a map's original creator.
