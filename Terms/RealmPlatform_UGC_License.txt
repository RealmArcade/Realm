Mirrors are Steam Workshop items and nothing else. There is no mirror site, mirror storage, mirror download page or public

cosmetic items is not a payment link and is not connected to any map.

Valve's rules prohibit commercial activity in Workshop content, including soliciting payment. A donation or payment

nothing purchasable may change gameplay, reach, information, matchmaking or randomness.
