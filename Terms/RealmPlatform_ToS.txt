1. WHAT REALM IS, AND WHAT IT IS NOT

Realm is a free strategy game with a map editor and a scripting system for maps. It runs on Steam.

- Steam provides the player's identity, the lobby directory, the peer-to-peer transport between players, the Workshop that
  hosts and distributes maps, the achievements, the statistics, the leaderboards, the cloud storage, the moderation of
  Workshop content, and the creator's public profile and Workshop pages.
- The developer of Realm does not operate player accounts, a profile service, a lobby directory, a map download service, a
  content delivery network, a relay or a game server. There is no Realm account, password or e-mail associated with
  playing the game.
- Realm's multiplayer path is Steam's. There is no direct-address connection, no forwarded port and no second network.

7. PURCHASES

The game is free. The only things the developer sells are cosmetic items of their own for the official game, through
Steam. Valve processes the payment and Valve's terms govern the transaction; the developer processes no payment, holds no

- No map, mode, feature, seat, unit, building or match option is purchasable.
- No creator content is sold, and no creator receives a share of any sale.
- A cosmetic item never affects gameplay, reach, information, matchmaking or randomness. Cosmetic assets ship inside the

8. CREATOR LINKS AND DONATIONS

The game displays no donation, payment, store or advertising link. It shows a Steam-managed purchase control for the
developer's own cosmetics, and, for a map, the optional Discord server invite that the map's creator declared in the map's
metadata. Neither is a link to a payment page.

A creator's own support channel is the community they run, linked from their Steam profile or their own Discord server.
Valve's rules prohibit commercial activity in Workshop content, including soliciting payment, so a donation request
belongs there rather than in a Workshop item or in the game. The creator is responsible for what they publish in either
place.

9. MODERATION AND TAKEDOWNS

The developer may hide, ban, mark incompatible or remove a mirror of UGC, and may report an item to Valve. Valve moderates
the Workshop.

A copyright complaint is made through the product's own reporting path: the end-of-match screen and the main menu open the
project's GitHub issue tracker, where a "DMCA/Copyright Infringement Report" issue is the intake. A report is reviewed, and
the item is hidden from the game's surfaces and reported to Valve where the report is upheld. Valve's own notice process
runs in parallel and is the process that can remove a Workshop item.

10. PRIVACY

Realm stores no account, password, e-mail, biography or profile copy. What it keeps locally is what the game needs to run:
settings, the map store, exported logs and, in a map's metadata, the creator's SteamID64, attribution tags and an optional
Discord invite. The optional announcements sign-up in the main menu sends the name and address you type to the e-mail
provider that delivers it, and nothing else; declining it changes nothing about the game. Diagnostics are exported only
when the player asks, and the SteamID is included only when the player ticks the box that says so.

Steam's privacy settings govern what your Steam profile and Workshop pages show. The creator can hide or delete their
Steam content through Steam.

11. CHANGES

These Terms are versioned. A material change takes effect with the release that carries it, and the version a map was
published under is recorded in that map's metadata. If you do not accept a change, your remedy is to stop using the
product; maps you have already exported remain yours and keep running offline.

Support, bug reports and takedown requests are handled through the project's GitHub repository. There is no support
